### 2026-10-13: Every page down to 320 px

- **Why**: the owner reported UI bugs. A sweep of every page on main's build at 320, 390, 768, and 1280 px (`docs/site/2026-10-13-ui-fixes/sweep.mjs`, output in `sweep-before.txt`) found two kinds. On `/bench`, each subject's heading in "Every lesson" sat in the version column (a subject group is an `li` too, and the rows' grid reached it), so on a wide screen the heading was 104 px wide and the model names ran over the first claim, and on a phone the rows were squeezed beside the heading. On phones, a claim's long dotted name (`durable_objects.code_update_strategy`, 36 characters) was kept on one line and widened the page: a lesson page was 433 px wide on a 390 px phone, and at 320 px 59 pages scrolled sideways: 52 of the 57 lesson pages, the home page, `/lessons`, `/wrangler/`, the teacher page, and the three model pages. The large seal (312 px) and source quotes and test captions with one long word did the same.
- **320 px is the floor**: WCAG 2.2 AA asks for reflow at 320 CSS px (1.4.10), and the site's budget is AA. An e2e test now loads every page at 320, 390, 768, and 1024 px and fails on any sideways scroll; another checks that each subject heading on `/bench` spans its rows, with the model names over their marks.
- **Flags and dotted names** (`Claim.astro`) still move to the next line whole, but break when one is wider than a whole line, at any width: a second sweep, after a phone-only first fix, found a 34-character flag 41 px too wide for the claim column of a replaced lesson's page at 1024 px (`sweep-first-round.txt`). They are no longer `nowrap`. A dotted name has no place to break in it, so it needs nothing; a flag would break after a hyphen, so each run of hyphens is held to the character after it (`-o`, `--d`) in a `nowrap` span, which adds no characters (copying a flag gives the flag). Each name may break anywhere else (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`), which a browser uses only when the name can't fit on a line of its own. Other places that print a claim or a long word without `Claim` (the corrected claims and the banner on a lesson page, the model pages' lists, source quotes, test captions) wrap anywhere.
- **The large seal** keeps one line where it fits; on a narrower line its word takes two lines and the block grows taller, instead of running off the screen.
