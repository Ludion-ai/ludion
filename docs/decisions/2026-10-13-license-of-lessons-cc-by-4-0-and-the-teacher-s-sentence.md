### 2026-10-13: License of lessons: CC BY 4.0, and the teacher's sentence

- **Decided by the owner, 2026-10-13: lessons are CC BY 4.0.** Code stays Apache-2.0 (a Creative Commons license is not for software; the owner's decision named the lessons' license, and nothing changes for code). It replaces CC BY-SA 4.0, the v0 spec's choice. Why, from the question as it stood in #60: `ludion sync` writes lessons into projects, private repositories among them, and a share-alike license would put the terms of the lessons on whatever file holds them.
- **Who it covers.** Every lesson on main was taught by one account (`author_id` 292738933, the owner's; each lesson says whether an agent drafted it), so changing to the more permissive license needs nobody else's agreement. From now on every public lesson comes with the sentence below.
- **The sentence.** `ludion teach --public` prints it before the `[y/N]` prompt (which now says "with the sentence above"), and puts it, quoted, in the pull request body: "I taught this lesson and I can share it. I license it under CC BY 4.0. I agree that Ludion may publish it on ludion.ai, in its index and feeds, and in the files `ludion sync` writes into projects, and that anyone may use it under that license, including to train or evaluate AI models, with credit to my GitHub account."
  - Why each part: "I can share it" because a quote or a test may come from somewhere else; the index, the feeds and `sync` are how every lesson is used; training and evaluation are named because a lesson is made to be read by models, and a teacher should not learn that afterwards.
  - **CLI prompt, not a checkbox.** A checkbox in a pull request template (`.github/`, protected) can't be checked by CI as read, and the prompt already needs a person at a terminal, with no flag to skip it. So there is no template change. This closes the question #60 left open.
- **Where it shows**: `LICENSE-LESSONS` (the CC BY 4.0 legal code, from creativecommons.org), the README, the CLI's README, the site footer. The CLI part is in the next release; until then `teach --public` shows no sentence. A lesson on main does not carry its license in its file: the repository's `LICENSE-LESSONS` is the license of every lesson in it.
- Replaces: #60 (the same sentence with `<LICENSE>` left open), closed as superseded.
