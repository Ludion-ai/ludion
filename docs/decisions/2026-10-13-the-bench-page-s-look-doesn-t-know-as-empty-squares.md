### 2026-10-13: The bench page's look: "doesn't know" as empty squares

- **Show the bit, not the word.** The per-lesson table carried one bit per cell as a word, "misses" in nearly every one. `/bench/` now leads with a **knowledge strip** per model: a square per active lesson, in subject rows. Filled: it knows the change without tools (models.json, both answers right). Outline only: it doesn't. Dotted: not measured (an active lesson models.json has no verdict for, so the gap between measured and all lessons shows instead of being left out). Beside it, a big "28 / 29" (measured lessons it doesn't know) with that share's Wilson interval. Hovering a square shows the claim's first sentence; clicking opens the lesson. The squares are out of the tab order and the accessibility tree; one sentence per model says the count, and the lesson list has every lesson as text.
- **Done 3** is two thick bars per model (without and with sync, share of B questions answered right) with the 95% interval as whiskers, and the difference in percentage points beside them, rounded from the same counts (+27). The per-subject table moved under "By subject".
- **Knowledge dates**: a dot per lesson at the day npm published the lowest version its range admits, per model, filled or hollow or dotted as above. Dates come from `docs/bench/release-dates.json`, written by `apps/site/scripts/release-dates.ts` from the registry's `time` and committed; the build never goes to the network. A lesson whose version isn't in the file yet, or that isn't an npm package, has no dot, and the page counts them. A model's knowledge cutoff is drawn only from a published source: the file's `cutoffs` hold the month, the URL, and the row it came from (Anthropic's models overview, "Reliable knowledge cutoff": Jun 2026 for all three, checked 2026-10-13). A stretch of more than 45 days with no dot, cutoff, or measuring day in it is cut short and drawn as a break in the axis, so one old lesson (the taught vitest 4.0.0 one, Oct 2025) doesn't squeeze the rest into a corner. Phones get the same chart turned on its side (time running down), not a sideways scroll that would open on the empty band.
- **Every lesson**: one line each (subject, versions in a monospace chip, kind, a "silent" mark, the claim's first sentence, and each model's mark at the right), with filters by subject, kind, and silent (a few lines of JavaScript; without it, every row shows). Links underline on hover only. The method, the rerun line, and the without-tools table are folded into a `<details>` at the end.
- **One hue, `--mark`** (tokens.css): the blue of deep water, `#0b6fa4` on white (5.5:1) and `#5ec2e8` on black (10.4:1). It marks measured data only (a known lesson, a measured share); it is never on chrome, and never means good or bad. Not measured is the gray of "checking". Squares light in order on load and the bars grow; both stop under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Share cards** are the strip and the count only; their squares shrink so the longest subject's row and every subject's row fit the 1200×630 card.
- **Labels follow the claims** (from the review): the list's "silent" mark and the "Silent only" filter leave out additions, as `generateClaim` does (for an addition the evidence shows only that older versions lack it), and every agent-drafted lesson says "Drafted by an agent" in the list and in its tooltips. The dates chart says in words how many lessons are dated and from when to when, since its dots are hidden from screen readers. The wide chart switches to the vertical one below 860 px, where its labels would get too small. A cutoff band is drawn across the chart when every model's cutoff is the same month, and per row otherwise. Each cutoff in `release-dates.json` keeps the page's row as it reads there and the page's definition of the term, so it can be checked offline. A test computes `--mark`'s contrast from tokens.css (at least 3:1 on the page and its panels, light and dark).
