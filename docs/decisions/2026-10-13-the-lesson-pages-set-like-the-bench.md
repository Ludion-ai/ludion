### 2026-10-13: The lesson pages, set like the bench

- **Why**: the owner asked for better lessons pages after the bench redesign. `/lessons` was 57 claims in one column at 26 px, each with the same signature line under it, and it scrolled sideways on a phone; a lesson page set its whole claim as one heading and printed a differential test's code twice.
- **A claim in parts** (`apps/site/src/build/claim-parts.ts`): the generated headline, the detail, and the closing sentence generateClaim adds ("Silent: …" or "Older versions don't have it."). Pages set them in different weights; joined, they are the claim word for word (a test checks every claim on the site).
- **A list row** (`LessonCard`, used on `/lessons`, the home page, subject pages, and teacher pages): the headline and detail on the left; on the right, in small type, the versions, the kind, a muted speaker for silent (the closing sentence is its title and is read to screen readers), and the signature with a compact seal (the word stays for screen readers and on hover). "Drafted by an agent" stays in words next to the teacher.
- **`/lessons`**: a tile per subject (its count, a bar against the largest, its newest day), how the lessons were verified (counted from index.json), a search box and filters (subject, kind, silent; without JavaScript every lesson shows), and the lessons grouped by subject with its name held on the left. Grouping by day was tried first and dropped: 55 of the 57 lessons were verified the same day.
- **A lesson page**: the subject, versions, and kind above the headline, then the detail and the closing sentence beside its mark; the panel adds whether each measured model knows the change without tools (models.json, as on /bench, or "Not measured yet"). A lesson core labels differential (`isDifferential`: same code and other pins, its own package inside and outside the range) shows the versions of its own package it runs on, oldest first, and what CI requires of each (must fail, printing the error the lesson names; must pass), then its code once when it is the same on every version. A pass/fail pair that isn't differential (another pin changes too; two lessons pin `@vitest/browser` or `@vitest/ui` along with vitest) is labeled "Verified by test" and gets no drawing, only its tests. (From the review of #106, which found the first version drawing those two with the wrong package.)
