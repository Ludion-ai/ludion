### 2026-10-14: The launch film (`video/`)

- **Why**: the owner asked for a launch film for X and the top of the site, with one idea: a change you can't see becomes one you can. Constraints from the brief: every answer, terminal line and number on screen comes from a record in the repo; music and effects made in code; it reads with the sound off; source in the repo, rendered frame by frame with one command; the MP4 not in Git.
- **The story, 30 s at 120 BPM**: "The software changed." → six real silent changes from `lessons/` (one per eighth note) ending on vitest's `extends` → "Silent." with the claim's own sentence ("Code written for older versions still runs, without an error.") → the recorded Done 3 answer without sync, struck through, "Confidently wrong." → `npx ludion-ai sync` typed into silence → the lesson as sync wrote it → the recorded answer with sync, "Correct." → 24/45 and 36/45 → the mark, the wordmark, the home page's headline, the command. One question all the way through (`vitest-5-0-0-14-b`), because it is the one the demo cast and the home page already use, its answers are short enough to read, and its lesson is a differential test.
- **The mark carries the plot**: 「 (the person) frames both answers from the start. The AI's 」 lands crooked on the wrong answer, flickers, and falls; on the right answer it slides in and locks on a downbeat; then both halves fly together into the logo. The red stays the AI's color, never a "wrong" color, and the site's blue appears only on the measured bars (Design B: no hue for good or bad).
- **The sound tells the same story**: under water at "Silent." (the band behind a low-pass, quieter, one sonar ping), dry under the answer, a tape stop into real silence at the stamp, the command typed into that silence, a riser and a snare roll into the drop on the lesson, a bell chord when the bracket locks. Bars start on odd seconds so the slam (1 s), the stamp (11 s) and the drop (15 s) are downbeats. Target −14 LUFS, true peak under −1 dBTP.
- **Tools**: a plain HTML page driven by `render(t)` and captured by Playwright's Chromium (already a dev dependency), not Remotion (its license terms depend on company size, which is the owner's call) and not a screen recording. The water is the site's shader on the film's clock. ffmpeg comes from `ffmpeg-static` in `video/package.json`, which is **not a workspace**: the root install and CI never download it.
- **Accuracy notes kept on screen**: each answer says "Recorded answer, graded …", the run, and "one trial per condition"; the with-sync note says "with the lessons ludion sync wrote", not `npx ludion-ai sync`, because that run used the CLI built from the repo with the frozen lessons; the sync scene says it was recorded on 2026-10-14 with ludion-ai 0.1.4. The montage, whose claims are all seed lessons, carries "Taught by @Ludion-ai, drafted by an agent", and so does the lesson card (from the line sync wrote).
- **Not decided here**: where the finished MP4s are kept and how the site serves one (an R2 bucket or a release asset needs the owner; `video/README.md`).
