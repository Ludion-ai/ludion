# The protected-paths check removes one fixed footer before it reads the last line (LP-core, 2026-10-29)

Fixed before the change is written. The owner's decision A of 2026-10-29 ("#181 でフッターを除いてから判定することを認める") allows it; the owner merges. It amends rule 2 of docs/decisions/2026-10-29-protected-paths-approval.md and nothing else.

## The rule

Some reviews are posted with a footer that the tool posting them appends after the review's own last line, so `reviewed-sha:` is no longer last. Before the last line is read:

1. Trailing whitespace of the whole body is removed, as before.
2. If the body's last two lines are exactly `---` and then `_Generated by [Claude Code](https://claude.ai/code)_`, each with only trailing whitespace (spaces, `\r`) after it, those two lines are removed, and then the trailing whitespace again, so blank lines between the review and the footer do not matter. Blank lines after the footer are covered by step 1.
3. Otherwise nothing is removed. A footer with any other line after it, a footer with a blank line or anything else between its two lines, leading spaces, a different link or wording, or only one of the two lines is not removed, so the comment's last line is not `reviewed-sha:` and it is no review.
4. Only one footer is removed. The first line and the verdict are read as before.

## Tests

Two tests are added to packages/cli/test/protected-paths-workflow.test.ts: an approving review with the footer (with and without blank lines around it, and with `\r\n`) passes; a footer with another line after it, or the footer altered, does not count. Mutations that break the removal must each fail at least one test (docs/bench/2026-10-29-lp-core/).

## Not changed

Who may review, what approves, the label, the latest-review rule and the wait table.
