# 2026-10-30 — A review has the line reviewed-sha:, it need not end with it

**Owner decision, 2026-10-30**, approved by the owner (「OK」): the rule on recording a review says that the review's comment has the line `reviewed-sha: <the head SHA reviewed>`, instead of that it ends with that line. This replaces the wording "ends with" of docs/decisions/2026-10-28-every-review-is-recorded-on-its-pr.md, which stays as it was; the rest of that decision is unchanged. CLAUDE.md (Engineering and security, Reviews) and docs/protected-paths.md (step 1 of how a protected PR gets approved, and "Every review is recorded on its PR") say it the same way.

## Why

When a review is posted, the environment adds a line `_Generated by [Claude Code](https://claude.ai/code)_` after it. Of the 24 review comments with a `reviewed-sha:` line on #209 to #215 posted up to 2026-10-30 09:47 UTC, 9 end with that footer, not with `reviewed-sha:` (docs/mistakes/2026-10-30-review-footer-after-reviewed-sha.md). Read word for word, those reviews broke the rule, though each holds its record. The required check `protected-paths` already reads every line of a comment for `reviewed-sha:` (`.github/workflows/protected-paths.yml`), so it is not changed.

## Decided

Only where the line stands is loosened. A review still has the line `reviewed-sha:` with the head SHA it reviewed, in a comment on its PR; its first line still states the verdict; a protected PR's review is still made with fresh context by someone other than the author and reads the full diff at the current head; any push still makes an earlier `reviewed-sha:` stop counting; and a review with no such comment on GitHub is still never reported as a review.

tools/records/test/records.test.ts fails when CLAUDE.md and docs/protected-paths.md no longer say the same.

## Undo

A later decision of the owner's, with its own record. Requiring the last line again needs the posting environment to stop adding its footer, or every review to be edited after it is posted.
