# 2026-11-02 — The water decision changed a rule after a result and said it only added measurement

- **What happened.** Two statements in PR #259 were stronger than the facts. A fresh-context review of the PR found them (PR comment 6565528619).
  1. **"Only adds measurement."** Before any contrast value was measured, rule 3 of `docs/decisions/2026-11-02-le2e-water-sampling.md` said "The moments of Arm C are then added to the check" (`git show c657a41`). After runs T0, A1, A2, B1 and B2 (`docs/bench/le2e/timing.json`), commit ec88fd3 put a chosen list in their place, in the section's "What is added to the check". The same section said "It adds measurement and keeps every rule above as written", and the commit's message said "It only adds measurement." (`git show ec88fd3`). That was a change of a rule after a result.
  2. **"Rules 6 and 7 do not apply."** The progress file at 5961fda said this and "No moment was dropped", and the commit's message said "No moment is dropped" (`git show 5961fda`). Those compare B's 240 moments, 70.1 s, with rule 5's 240 s. With Arm C as it was run, the check's runs sum to 348.562 s (`totalMs` in `docs/bench/le2e/runs/b1.json` and `docs/bench/le2e/runs/c.json`, two separate runs added, not one run). Rule 5 is then met, so a shorter list had to meet rule 7, and it does not: nothing in `e2e/` compares the boxes or the shader with those measured (`e2e/water-contrast.ts`, `e2e/water.spec.ts`).

  No result moved by this: on the site measured, the final list is higher than A1, A2 and A3 on 0 of 152 boxes, and below its threshold on 0 (`docs/bench/le2e/minimums.csv`). What became weaker is the number of moments fixed for a changed site, 246 against 1,509 (`docs/bench/le2e/runs/f1.json`, and B's and C's rows).
- **Why.**
  1. The sentence did not come from reading the section under it. The sentence and the change it denies are in one section of one commit (`git show ec88fd3`).
  2. "Loosened" was judged by values on the site measured. Every condition the section puts on the list is of that kind ("No box's minimum may rise by this choice", Arm F, Arm A3), and at ec88fd3 it names neither rule 5 nor rule 7 (`git show ec88fd3`). So it had nothing to say about a site that changes later.
  3. The choice was shown to the reader and not written into the record. The PR body said "Please look at this choice" from its first version (the PR's edit history), while the decision's text did not change from ec88fd3 to bcecdcc (`git diff ec88fd3 bcecdcc`, empty for that file).
  4. No record came with the correction. The lane's brief had said none was needed, since the change fixes no bug, and that was written before this mistake. ee3e2de called the statements wrong and added no file here (`git diff d42e172 ee3e2de`, empty for docs/mistakes). A second review found that (PR comment 6566508933).
