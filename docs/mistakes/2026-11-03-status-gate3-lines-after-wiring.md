# 2026-11-03 — docs/status.md kept two old lines on gate 3, and gained one stronger than its evidence

- **What happened.** The review of #279 at ccbb6e9 found three sentences in docs/status.md. That PR makes npm's `patch` and `watch` call gate 3's first form, and says so in the file's section "Gate 3's new type". Two older lines still said otherwise: "A first form of these exists and nothing calls it", and "Gate 3's comparison of GET pages: not built (`crawl`: 0)." The third was new in that PR, and its description said the same: a Next.js project that reads `next/font/google` cannot build in the box. No test or measurement shows that. Gate 3's definition has the claim and says it came from an audit and was not measured, and the new sentence dropped that.
- **Why.** The search for sentences to change was case-sensitive and used the words of the one line it already knew ("Nothing calls"), so it did not find "nothing calls it" or "not built (`crawl`". It was not a reading of every line that names gate 3. The `next/font/google` sentence was written as a consequence worth warning about, taken from the definition without its last clause, and was not traced to a run.
- **What changed.** The first two lines now say that it is called, and the first also says that a project whose own test does not pass on old does not reach it. The third sentence is gone from docs/status.md and from the PR's description. The definition keeps its line, with its caveat.
- No check catches this kind of mistake yet. The rule below is the one this record is kept under, not one that prevents the mistake.
- **Rule:** "docs/status.md holds what is built, what is missing and what waits for the owner; a PR that changes what Ludion does updates it in the same PR."
