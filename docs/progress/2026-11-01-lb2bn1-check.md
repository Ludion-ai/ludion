## Limits found while building it

- Composer: Packagist returns its own advisory ids (PKSA-) beside OSV's; the check does not send those to OSV, so a Composer fix with one is always due, as before this change. Reading the PKSA record's GHSA alias would let the anchor be known; not done.
- A KEV addition moves t* earlier but not the anchor, so a waiting npm fix whose urgency rises late is due at the next due slot after the new t*, not at once (within the first 36 hours after t* it is due at once).
- A fix whose wait the judge extends beyond t*, a commit whose time is older than its push, an OSV change that leaves `modified` alone, and a due slot GitHub does not run all wait for the next due slot (definitions, 5).
