# 2026-11-01 (LDGLAT) — How long a Debian security fix takes to reach the official node and php tags (bookworm, past year)

A measurement that only reads; no product code, gate, wait or workflow changed. Definitions: `docs/decisions/2026-11-01-ldglat-definitions.md`, committed (8c8800d2f1bf1585ccea81e32f43124f971a5ac0) before counting. Detail: `docs/bench/2026-11-01-ldglat/summary.md` (numbers copied from `results.md`, which the analysis script prints). docker-library/repo-info (at 2427e432, 2026-10-31T14:59:06Z) and snapshot.debian.org were read only inside a container with no credentials; no image was pulled or run.

- **Fixes:** 69 bookworm security-archive uploads in 2025-11-01 to 2026-10-31 for packages of the counted images; **10** of them were for a package debian:bookworm-slim (and debian:bookworm) had at the time (units), ended by **6** distinct base-image rebuilds. Small sample.
- **Fix → official tag (total), median 202.4 h (8.4 days) for the 8 tags on debian:bookworm-slim** (tags × units: n 72 reached, 8 not reached by the data end; p90 553.2, max 585.7); per unit, median across its tags, also 202.4. Tags on debian:bookworm (node:22/24-bookworm): 180.4. node:22/24-bookworm-slim alone: 353.9; php tags: 202.4.
- **Fix → base image (stage 1), median 336.7 h by repo-info's commit, 299.8 h by the image's `Created`** (n 9 reached, 1 not reached).
- **Base image → tag (stage 2) is mostly negative** (median −14.9 h on bookworm-slim, 52 of 72 below zero). The tags' own `apt-get install` pulls some fixes before the base has them, and repo-info's commit for the debian file can lag the push by days (readings from the rows). So the two-stage split does not hold with these sources. The total does not depend on it.
- **Rebuild intervals (new Image ID):** debian:bookworm-slim median 432 h (min 168, 21 rebuilds, 9 with a package change); node and php tags medians 240 to 335.9 h, min about 70.6 to 72 h. Intervals under 72 h: 0 to 3 of 20 to 35 per tag; none under 24 h. With a 72 h wait from a tag's newest digest, a rebuild would rarely restart LDG's wait. Where the wait starts is not decided here.
- **Not measured:** Alpine, trixie, arm64, point-release fixes, the images' push times (repo-info gives the time a scan saw a change, at about a day's resolution, scan interval 不明). The Alpine method and a forward hourly record (its own workflow and concurrency group, apart from shadow.yml) are designed in the decision's section 8 and not built.
- **Changed after the first run** (listed in summary.md): a `srcfiles` fallback for 7 snapshot 404s (all 7 were not security uploads), and leaving out 2 bullseye `~deb11u1` versions (8 secondary units; base-image units unchanged).
