# LMVN: Maven out of `patch`, and the public sentences (2026-11-01)

Built from main 4aefb22. The scope change is the owner's (2026-11-01 13:09 JST, the one sentence 「Ludion は Maven の依存を直さなくなる（読むだけの確かめは残す）」; docs/decisions/2026-11-01-definition-of-complete.md).

## What changed

- `MAVEN_REFUSAL` and `refuseMavenPolicy` in packages/cli/src/security/project.ts. `readConfig` still reads a Maven policy, because scan takes its tool image from it.
- `patchDependencies` refuses a Maven policy before its snapshot, OSV or Docker. So `ludion patch` does, with exit 2 and the reason on stderr (`ludion: maven is read-only: ...`), and with `--json` too.
- `ludion watch` stops at the refusal, since it would otherwise print it every round for good.
- `init`, `patch` and `watch` refuse `--ecosystem maven`; `init` refuses a project whose only inventory is a pom.xml, and writes no policy.
- The PR job (`policyAt` in deliver.ts) refuses an artifact when the base commit's policy names Maven.
- `ludion check` still leaves a Maven policy undecided, so the validation job runs and fails with the reason, rather than the job staying green while nothing is fixed. Its reason says so.
- `ludion scan` is unchanged. Its `Supported ecosystems` error, the CLI's help and the package description now say Maven is scan only.
- README.md and packages/cli/README.md (byte for byte the same, as before), apps/site/src/strings/en.ts and ja.ts, apps/site/src/build/discovery.ts (/llms.txt) and docs/status.md.
- No file is deleted or renamed. Maven's code in dependency-patch.ts, inventory.ts, acquisition.ts and deliver.ts stays, now reached by scan only (the scan's graph) or by nothing (the fix path). Whether and when to remove it is not decided.

## Kept on the site and in the READMEs, because the scan still does it

The Maven row in the supported-scope table, with its boundary; that scan resolves the graph in a manifest-only container; that Maven repositories are mirrored to public Central in "What leaves your machine".
